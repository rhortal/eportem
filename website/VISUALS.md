# Where the site's pictures come from

Every picture on the public site is drawn in HTML and CSS, not a screenshot. Each one
is a partial in `src/` (`_v-<name>.<lang>.html`, English and Spanish) and starts with a
`<!-- visual: ... Source: ... -->` comment naming what it is drawn from. This file is
the catalogue: when the product's UI changes or gets a redesign, use it to find which
pictures to redraw, and note here what each one simplifies or invents.

Rules that hold for every picture:

- **Pictures of CheckIn** copy the product's own screens and colours (the palette in
  `src/assets/site.css` is the one in `checkin/web/static/style.css`). Keep them faithful
  enough that a person who signs up recognises what they see.
- **Pictures of ePortem** are never screenshots and never copy its design, logo,
  colours or wording. They are generic drawings of "a time-recording page", marked
  "Illustration" on the page itself. Only one exists (`schedule-to-record`).
- **No third-party apps' designs** either: the phone is a generic phone, not Telegram's
  or Slack's interface.
