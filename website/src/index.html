How it works

Set it up once. After that, the only time you think about clocking in is when you take a day off.

  1. Pick your week

    Choose a working-day template, or set your own times for each day, and mark which days you work from home.

  2. Connect your account

    Give CheckIn your ePortem login. It's stored encrypted and used only to record your clock events.

  3. Get on with your day

    CheckIn clocks you in, out for lunch, back and out at the end of the day, and tells you each time.

Never late in. Never early out.

Each clock event lands at a slightly different moment every day, like a person's would. But the day never shrinks: the clock-in always comes before your start time and the clock-out always after your stop time.

Questions

Is CheckIn part of ePortem?

No. CheckIn is an independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by ePortem or its owner. It works with your own ePortem account, the way you would through your browser.

Can I use it at my company?

Check your company's rules first. Some companies don't allow automated tools, and it's your responsibility to follow their policies. In Spain the working-time record is a legal obligation, and it must show the hours you actually work.

Will it record hours I didn't work?

It records what your schedule says, so keep your schedule true: mark time off, sick days and changes. CheckIn is for people who work their hours and don't want to forget to clock them, not for recording hours that weren't worked.

What if a clock event fails?

You get an alert straight away, and the end-of-day check lists anything missing. Then you fix it on ePortem by hand. CheckIn never guesses: if it isn't sure an action was recorded, it says so rather than trying again.

What if my company uses a different system?

CheckIn works with ePortem today. If your company records working time on another system, tell us which one: it helps us decide what to support next.

When can I use it, and what will it cost?

CheckIn is in its final testing, and a small number of people can join early. Places are limited, so if you'd like one, send us a message. Pricing isn't decided yet.
