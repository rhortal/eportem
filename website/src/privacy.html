The CheckIn service, once it opens

To clock in for you, CheckIn will process:

  • your CheckIn account: username, email, language and time zone;
  • your ePortem username and password, encrypted at rest, used only to sign in to ePortem for you, and the ePortem session that sign-in creates;
  • your schedule: working hours, home and office days, time off, business trips and day changes;
  • the history of your clock events and their results, so you and we can check what happened;
  • the notification channels you add (such as a Telegram chat or Slack workspace), with their access keys encrypted.

We use this only to provide CheckIn to you (legal basis: the contract with you, GDPR art. 6.1.b). We don't sell it, use it for advertising, or share it, except with ePortem and the messaging services you connect, as needed to do what you ask, and with providers that host CheckIn for us under a data processing agreement. We keep it while your account is open and delete it within 30 days of you closing it, except what the law requires us to keep.

Your schedule and clock events are working-time data about you, which your employer also holds. CheckIn does not send anything to your employer other than what ePortem records when you clock in.
