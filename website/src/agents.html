CheckIn for AI agents

In short. CheckIn comes with a skill for AI agents (Claude Code, or any agent that uses skills). Ask it things like "what's my plan for tomorrow?", "I'm off from 24 to 31 December", "I'll work from home on Thursday" or "clock me in now", and it does them in your CheckIn account. It signs in with a link you approve, and never needs or sees your password.

What it can do

  • Tell you today's plan, the coming days, your schedule and the history of clock events, and whether one went through.
  • Change single days (time off, home or office, other hours, business trips), your usual week, periods such as summer hours, and local holidays; pause and resume CheckIn.
  • Clock in, go to lunch, come back or clock out right now, as you would in your time-recording site: only when you ask, after you confirm the action and the place.
  • It leaves your ePortem account, passwords and keys alone unless you ask for exactly that.

How to get it

The skill is part of your CheckIn account. Once you have access, sign in to CheckIn and open the API tokens page. Its "For AI agents" box has a text to copy into your agent's chat: the agent installs the skill, signs in and shows you a link. Open it, check the code, choose what the agent may do (only look, or also make changes) and approve.

The agent gets its own key, valid for 90 days, which you can revoke at any time on the same page. The skill keeps itself up to date from your CheckIn account.
